## 1. Core Corrections (What we FIXED from the old design)

| **Old Mistake (Zenith)**                    | **GDPTraders Fix**                                                                                                          |
|:------------------------------------------- |:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Placeholder Stats** ($0M, $0B)            | **Removed entirely.** We show a "Track Record" chart (X-axis: time, Y-axis: growth) vs. Bitcoin benchmark. No fake numbers. |

| **Hidden Fees**                             | **Full Fee Transparency** displayed immediately on the pricing page.                                                        |
| **Missing Legal Text**                      | **Massive Risk Warning** at the top of every investment page.                                                               |

## 2. Branding & Tone

- **Voice:** Authoritative, Calm, Data-Driven. (Never "Get Rich Quick").
- **Color Palette:** 
  - Primary: Deep Navy (#0A1128) - Represents stability.
  - Accent: Bitcoin Orange (#F7931A) - Represents innovation.
  - Highlight: Cool Mint (#00F2FE) - Represents transparency/liquidity.
- **Typography:** Clean, sans-serif (Inter or Roboto) for high readability on financial data.

## 10. Technical "Anti-Scam" Requirements

1. **No Pop-up Deposits:** Do not ask for money immediately upon page load. Let them read the white paper first.
2. **SSL & Security:** Ensure the site uses HSTS and strict CSP headers.
3. **External Audits:** Publish a link to a recent smart contract/security audit (e.g., by CertiK or Trail of Bits) if DeFi strategies are used.
4. **Transparent Withdrawals:** Clearly state withdrawal processing times (e.g., 1-3 business days for fiat, instant for crypto to whitelisted wallets).
